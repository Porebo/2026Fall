Jarvis Spiritual Preparation

5-Day Preparation Plan for Stake Conference

A simple plan to invite more of the Spirit before stake conference.

Day 1 - Set Your Intent (Come unto Christ)

  • Pray and ask: "What does the Lord want me to do after stake conference?"
  • Read 3 Nephi 11:1-17, focusing on "Arise and come forth unto me."
  • Write one question you are bringing to conference.
  • Do one simple act of repentance: apologize, fix something, or let something go.

Day 2 - Learn How the Church Is Led (Councils)

  • Study General Handbook 4.3 (Councils).
  • Pray for your stake leaders by name: the stake presidency, high council, and bishops.
  • In the evening, spend 5 minutes in quiet and note any impressions.

Day 3 - Invite Revelation (Effective Councils and Unity)

  • Study General Handbook 4.4 (Principles of Effective Councils).
  • Reach out to one person you minister to, or someone who may be drifting, with a sincere message.
  • Pray: "Help me recognize the Spirit quickly and obey promptly."

Day 4 - Follow the Request (Leadership Instruction and Area Guidance)

  • In Gospel Library, read the April 2026 Leadership Instruction Summaries. Take three bullet notes: what, why, and what we will do.
  • Review the US West Area "Welcome Meetings" guidance and write down one action you can help implement.
  • If possible, attend the temple or set aside 20-30 minutes for quiet scripture and prayer time without your phone.

Day 5 - Spiritual Readiness (Simple and Powerful)

  • If you are able, fast, even partially, specifically for stake conference.
  • Read Moroni 10:3-5 and pray for the gift to hear Him during conference.
  • Prepare to arrive early, sit where you can focus, bring a notebook, and commit to write one impression per talk or session.

This plan can be tailored for a quorum presidency, bishopric, ministering brothers supervisor, or ward mission leader, especially around the "Welcome Meetings" responsibility.
