Request for Graduate Advisor: Master's in Information Science

Letter sent to prospective faculty advisors for the final two semesters of the Master's program.

Date sent: August 27, 2026

Dear Professor,

I'm writing to ask if you'd consider serving as my graduate advisor for my final two semesters of my Master's in Information Science. I'm planning to complete either a graduate project or thesis, and I believe your work in data quality aligns closely with my research interests.

My career over the past twenty years has given me deep, practical insight into data challenges in enterprise environments. Working in oil and gas at Bakersfield, I've built a foundation in geology-related data systems and have developed strong proficiency in SQL and application development. I now augment this work with AI-assisted programming, and I've recently transitioned my organization from Tableau to Spotfire. I'm confident in navigating both platforms to create meaningful analytics.

My research direction centers on data quality governance in technical environments. I'm particularly interested in how organizations can build sustainable, scalable approaches to data integrity as they modernize their tools and practices. Your expertise in this area would be invaluable as I shape my thesis or project.

For the graduate project course, if you don't have a section available, I understand we can enroll through Mihail Tudoreanu's section while you serve as my advisor and set my grade at the end.

Beyond my immediate goal of completing the Master's degree, I'm committed to pursuing a PhD in data quality. I see my graduate work as the foundation for a long-term research agenda that bridges industry practice and academic rigor.

I would be grateful for the opportunity to work with you. I'm happy to discuss my background and research direction at your convenience.

Best regards,
Lester S. Carcamo
Master's Student
