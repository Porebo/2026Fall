Public Resources Code - PRCDIVISION 3. OIL AND GAS [3000 - 3865]  ( Division 3 enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93. )  CHAPTER 1. Oil and Gas Conservation [3000 - 3473]  ( Chapter 1 enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93. )  ARTICLE 4. Regulation of Operations [3200 - 3238]  ( Article 4 enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93. )  3216.  The owner or operator of any well, or his local agent, shall file with the supervisor a copy of the log, history, and core record, or any portion thereof, at any time after the commencement of the work of drilling any well upon written request of the supervisor, or the district deputy. The request shall be signed by the supervisor, or the district deputy, and served either personally, or by mailing a copy of the request, by registered mail, to the last known post office address of the owner or operator, or his agent.(Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1073.)
