HW07 — Week 10 Assignment (Research Proposal Planning)

Research Proposal Planning Assignment

This assignment is about project management for research: take the first steps to report your progress to date to Dr. Wu and Dr. Dagtas by preparing a 20-minute progress presentation (7 to 10 slides) and submitting the slides to the instructors.

Assignment Instructions

Prepare a Progress Presentation

Review the activities needed to complete the research proposal, and consider building a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) for the phases and activities you need to finish by the end of the semester. Cover these items:

  1. Title slide for the research proposal: name, title, date.
  2. Summary of the problem/challenge you will address and the research questions you plan to focus on.
  3. Your research objectives.
  4. Literature review slide(s): progress, search terms, types of information sought, collections searched, examples of the best articles found so far (Google Folder with articles collected to date).
  5. Your research methods for collecting and analyzing data.
  6. Contributions (merits) of the research: who would benefit or find it useful.
  7. Remaining work to complete the research proposal.

Presentations start November 9 and continue November 16 and 23 as needed; students are assigned a date and may voluntarily present for feedback. If you cannot attend a session, submit your slides. Points are awarded once the assignment is completed in class.
