Method

Study Design, Sample, and Runs

Three prompts (naive, informed, chain-of-thought) ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini whether a casing diagram meets 14 CCR § 1724.7.1. The instrument is checklist v4.1: 90 presence-only points, 78 from subdivisions (a) and (d) and 12 from (b) and (c); a diagram's denominator is 90, or 89 when only one borehole is shown. The sample is 30 UIC injector diagrams, U01–U30, dated on or after April 1, 2019.
