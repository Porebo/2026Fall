CHANGE NOTE (2026-11-18, v4.0 -> v4.1)

Student decision: § 1724.7.1(b)(2) requires the slurry information "for each
cement job completed in each well," so v4.1 adds a scored point for cement
job identification: the date, stage number, or job name that identifies each
cement job (e.g., U01 shows "8/1/2013" and "8/11/2013" on its cement
entries). It is inserted as the first (b)(2) point, new point 81, "Cement job
identification (date or stage)". It is non-depth and has no TVD pair. Former
points 81-89 are renumbered 82-90; points 1-80 are unchanged. Total points:
90 (78 from subdivisions (a) and (d), 12 from (b) and (c)); 64 MD/non-depth
(37 non-depth, 27 MD) and 26 TVD. The conditional borehole point is now
point 90, so the denominator is 90, or 89 when only one borehole is shown.
The wellbore-path MD point (now 87) is still the only MD point without a TVD
pair. Database: requirement_id 90, (b)(2).cement_job_identification; see
migrate_v41_cement_job_identification.sql. Point numbers in the v4.0 note
below are v4.0 numbers.

EARLIER CHANGE NOTE (2026-11-18, v3.0 -> v4.0)

v4 adds 11 presence-only points, 79-89, from § 1724.7.1(b) and (c), in a new
section "Subdivisions (b) and (c)" after point 78. Points 1-78 are unchanged
in number, code, and label. Until v3 the (b) and (c) content was recorded only
as supporting another requirement and was not scored; from v4 it is scored.
Total points: 89 (63 MD/non-depth, 26 TVD). The 63 are 36 non-depth points
and 27 MD points; point 86 is the only MD point without a TVD pair, because
§ 1724.7.1(d) requires TVD only for depths required under subdivision (a).
The (b) codes reuse the 10 supporting-documentation requirement records
already in casing_specs.db (requirement_id 52-61); point 89 is new

Point 89 is conditional (student decision, 2026-11-18). § 1724.7.1(c)
applies only "when multiple boreholes are drilled in a well" and does not
require a label. Point 89 is the only point that may take the status
NOT_APPLICABLE: when the diagram shows a single borehole, point 89 is
NOT_APPLICABLE and is removed from that diagram's denominator, which is
then 88; otherwise the denominator is 89. The row count stays 89. See
migrate_v4b_point89_conditional.sql.

CHANGE NOTE (2026-11-18, v2.0 -> v3.0)

§ 1724.7.1(a)(10) requires "information regarding equipment in the well such
as subsurface safety valves, packers, and gas lift mandrels." Because "such as"
is illustrative, not exhaustive, v3 adds point 32, (a)(10).other_equipment,
for any other in-well equipment (e.g., tubing, pumps, rods). It is a non-depth,
presence-only point (no TVD pair), handled like point 28. Former points 32-77
are renumbered 33-78. Total points: 78 (52 MD/non-depth, 26 TVD). Point 32 is
MET when the diagram supplies information on in-well equipment other than
SSSVs, packers, and gas-lift mandrels, including an explicit "none" or "N/A";
a blank label is MISSING. Specification codes are unchanged for all other
points.

DECISION RULE

For each point, report MET when the diagram supplies an answer, including a
value, "none", "N/A", or another explicit entry. Report MISSING only when no
answer is supplied. Do not judge truth, quality, plausibility, engineering
validity, or regulatory adequacy. The single exception is point 90, which is
NOT_APPLICABLE when the diagram shows only one borehole (see Subdivisions (b)
and (c)). No other point may be marked NOT_APPLICABLE.

Return one row for every point below. Repeated structures must be represented:
when a casing, interval, job, borehole, formation, or feature appears more than
once, use the Displayed Answer cell to list every represented instance.

SUBDIVISIONS (b) AND (c)

Presence rule and limitation for (b). Subdivision (b) requires each casing
diagram to be "accompanied by documentation" of cement calculations, cement
slurry information, and the wellbore path. The study observes only the
published casing-diagram file being scored; documentation filed separately
with CalGEM is not seen. A (b) point (79-89) is MET if the information
appears on the scored diagram file, including "none", "N/A", or another
explicit entry, or if the diagram states or shows that the information for
that point accompanies it separately (e.g., "cement calculations attached",
"see directional survey", "slurry details in cement report"; student
decision, 2026-11-18). It is MISSING when the diagram neither shows nor
references the information. A MISSING (b) point therefore means "neither
shown nor referenced on the published diagram", not that the operator failed
to file the documentation.

Repeated instances. Points 79-86 apply to every cement job represented on the
diagram (each cement fill behind casing, each cement plug, or any other
cement-job entry). Points 87-90 apply to every borehole represented (original
hole, redrill, or sidetrack). Each point is MET only when every represented
instance has an answer; list every instance in Displayed Answer.

Point rules. For every point 79-89, any indication of the item is MET,
including "none" or "N/A"; a blank label or no mention is MISSING. The rules
below say only which point an indication answers.
79: MET when the steps of a cement-yield calculation are shown (inputs and
    the computation leading to the yield). A yield value alone is point 84,
    not point 79.
80: MET when the steps of any other cement calculation are shown (e.g.,
    annular volume, excess, sacks required). A result alone is not the steps.
81: MET when every cement job shown is identified by a date, stage number,
    or job name (e.g., "8/1/2013", "Stage 1", "Primary", "Squeeze"),
    including "N/A"; blank or not mentioned is MISSING (student decision,
    2026-11-18).
82: MET when a slurry volume or the quantity used to calculate it (e.g.,
    sacks, cubic feet, barrels) is shown for the job. A sack count alone
    is MET (student decision, 2026-11-18).
83: MET when a slurry density or weight (e.g., ppg, lb/cu ft) is shown.
84: MET when a slurry yield (e.g., cu ft/sk) is shown.
85: MET when a cement type or class (e.g., "Class G", "Class H") is shown.
86: MET when additives are listed or the slurry is stated to have none
    (e.g., "neat", "no additives").
87-89: MET when the wellbore path supplies the quantity: survey or path
    stations in measured depth (87), inclination (88), azimuth (89). An
    explicit statement that the hole is vertical or straight, or "N/A",
    answers all three. A deviation statement or range without stations
    (e.g., "Deviated (15° to 30°)") answers point 88 only; points 87 and 89
    are MISSING unless shown elsewhere.
90: Conditional. § 1724.7.1(c) applies only "when multiple boreholes are
    drilled in a well."
    - Diagram shows more than one borehole (original hole plus a redrill or
      sidetrack): point 90 is scored. MET when each bore is identified
      (e.g., "Original hole", "OH", "RD1", "ST01", wellbore "00"/"01") and
      the required information is given for each bore; otherwise MISSING.
    - Diagram shows a single borehole: NOT_APPLICABLE. No label is required.
      The point is removed from this diagram's denominator (90 -> 89).
    NOT_APPLICABLE is a status, not a displayed answer; an "N/A" written on
    the diagram is a displayed answer and does not by itself make point 90
    NOT_APPLICABLE. Subdivision (c) also applies every other point to each
    borehole, under the repeated-instance rule.

No TVD rows are added for points 79-90: § 1724.7.1(d) requires TVD only for
depths required under subdivision (a).
