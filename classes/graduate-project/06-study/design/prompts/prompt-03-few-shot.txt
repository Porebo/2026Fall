PROMPT 03 - FEW-SHOT
Version 1.3, November 13, 2026
Change in 1.3: standard changed from 14 CCR 1772.1.3 (idle wells) to 14 CCR 1724.7.1 (underground injection control), which has the same 15 items: it sits in the Onshore Well Regulations, governs every well in an injection project's area of review that penetrates the injection zone, producers included, and is the section CalGEM enforced in Order 1517. Its item (15) lacks the "if applicable" of 1772.1.3; the injection-zone clause is treated as not applicable to wells outside an injection project.
Change in 1.2: standard changed from 14 CCR 1726.4.1 (underground gas storage wells, 18 items) to 14 CCR 1772.1.3 (idle wells, 15 items; the same items as 14 CCR 1724.7.1 for injection project wells), the closer fit for the sampled oil and gas wells. The dry runs of November 11, 2026, used the earlier standard and prompt wording.

How the examples were built
---------------------------
The examples are derived from the wording of 14 CCR 1724.7.1, not from any
diagram. Each describes a made-up diagram with invented values and applies one
requirement's wording to it. No example uses a sampled diagram (W01-W30) or any
other real diagram, so the examples can neither reveal answers for the diagrams
under test nor favor one operator's drawing template.

Each example illustrates one way a requirement's wording decides the judgment:

  Example     Regulation wording                       What it illustrates
  (a)(1)      "Operator name, lease name, well number, a requirement listing
              and API number of the well"              several items is met only
                                                       when every item is shown
  (a)(2)      "Date the well was spudded"               a single item shown is met
  (a)(12)     "...including locations of the top and   showing part of a
              bottom of cement plugs"                  requirement (the top)
                                                       without the rest (the
                                                       bottom) is not met
  (d)         "Measured depth and true vertical depth   "and" requires both; one
              shall be provided for all depths"        depth reference alone is
                                                       not met
  (a)(15)     "...the injection zone and confining     a clause about the
              layer(s) for the underground injection   injection project does not
              project(s)"                              apply to a well outside
                                                       such a project

The three judgments used, Meets, Does not meet, and Not applicable, follow from
the regulation's own structure: "shall at a minimum include all of the
following data" (met or not met) and provisions limited to certain wells
(the injection project clause of (a)(15), which has nothing to apply to in a
well outside such a project, and (c) for wells with multiple boreholes), which
are not applicable to other wells.

The examples are written as a person would type them at a keyboard: short
sentences of the form "If a diagram shows ..., then (requirement) does not
meet," introduced with "Here's how I want each requirement judged. These are
made-up examples, not from the attached diagram." They are stated to be
made up so the AI does not confuse them with the attached diagram. The output
requested from the AI is still a table, as in level 2; only the examples are
in sentences.
Change log:
1.0  November 11, 2026   Examples derived from the regulation text, with
     invented values, presented as a table. An earlier draft that took example
     rows from a real diagram outside the sample (V.L. & W. 118) was rejected:
     the prompt is to be based on the regulation, not on diagrams, and that
     diagram shared a drawing template with several sampled diagrams.
1.1  November 11, 2026   Examples rewritten from a table into plain sentences,
     because a worker typing a request does not write tables. Content and
     judgments unchanged.
1.2  November 12, 2026   Examples re-derived from 14 CCR 1772.1.3 after the
     standard changed (see the note at the top). Each example keeps its
     purpose; item letters became numbers, the plug example now turns on the
     missing bottom depth (1772.1.3 does not require plug dates), and the
     not-applicable example now uses the "if applicable" clause of (a)(15).
1.3  November 13, 2026   Standard changed to 14 CCR 1724.7.1 (see the note at
     the top). The five examples are unchanged; item numbers are the same in
     both sections. The not-applicable example now rests on the logic of the
     injection project clause, since 1724.7.1 has no "if applicable."
