Public Resources Code - PRC DIVISION 3. OIL AND GAS [3000 - 3865] ( Division 3 enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93. ) CHAPTER 1. Oil and Gas Conservation [3000 - 3473] ( Chapter 1 enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93. ) ARTICLE 4. Regulation of Operations [3200 - 3238] ( Article 4 enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93. )

3210.
The owner or operator of any well shall keep, or cause to be kept, a careful and accurate log, core record, and history of the drilling of the well.
(Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
