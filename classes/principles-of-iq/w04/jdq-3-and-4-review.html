JDQ 3 and 4 Review

Review questions on Journey to Data Quality, Chapter 3 (Assessing Data Quality, Part I) and Chapter 4 (Assessing Data Quality, Part II). Chapter 4 problems are calculation based — use the metric formulas shown in the book and show the steps of each calculation.

Chapter 3 — Assessing Data Quality, Part I

  1. 3.1 Answer the following:
    1. What do the letters in the acronym TDQM stand for?
    2. List and explain the steps in the TDQM process.
  2. 3.2 Explain the three steps of the Comparative Approach: Subjective and Objective Assessments.
  3. 3.3 In the Comparative Approach:
    1. What does it mean if the comparison of the survey assessment and the metric assessment of an information quality dimension falls in Quadrant II of the Diagnostic Matrix?
    2. Select a dimension of data quality (from the Wang–Strong Framework) and a type of data (for example, sales transactions or product master data), then imagine a situation where the assessment of this data might fall into Quadrant II.
  4. 3.4 Explain two types of Gap Analyses that can be done from the results of the Information Quality Assessment Survey.
  5. 3.5 List and explain the steps in the “process-embedded data integrity” approach to information quality assessment.

Chapter 4 — Assessing Data Quality, Part II

  1. 4.1 List the Codd Integrity Constraints.
  2. 4.2 Calculate the Free-of-error rating where:
    • Total number of data units = 12,500
    • Number of data units in error = 475
  3. 4.3 Calculate the Appropriate-amount-of-data rating where:
    • Number of data units provided = 1,600
    • Number of data units needed = 1,200
  4. 4.4 Calculate the Timeliness rating where:
    • Currency = 3 hours
    • Volatility = 5 hours
    • Sensitivity = 0.5
  5. 4.5 Calculate the aggregate rating of the Free-of-error, Appropriate-amount-of-data, and Timeliness ratings from problems 4.2, 4.3, and 4.4, in two different ways:
    1. As the weighted average, using the weights:
      • Free-of-error weight = 0.30
      • Appropriate-amount-of-data weight = 0.20
      • Timeliness weight = 0.50
    2. Using the Minimum rating.
