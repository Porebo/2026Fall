DATASET OVERVIEW

The dataset contains information about shows while maintaining a cohesive theme of describing show attributes without off-topic noise.

Each row represents one information record for a show containing 13 attribute columns divided into three major categories: 2 record identification fields (show_id, date_added), 9 intrinsic show descriptors, and 2 performance measures (user_rating_score, number_of_reviews).


SCHEMA TAXONOMY

Metadata
  - Record & System Identification: show_id, date_added

Dimensional
  - Core Info & Format: title, type, description, duration
  - Production: director, country, release_year
  - Taxonomy & Rating: rating, listed_in

Fact
  - Performance Measures: user_rating_score, number_of_reviews
