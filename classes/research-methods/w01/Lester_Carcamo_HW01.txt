1. There are many definitions for research. In a sentence or two, how would you explain to someone what is the meaning and purpose of research?

Research is an activity that we may engage in without even thinking about it, or it may be motivated by a need for information. 5. What area, topic, issue, problem, or challenge are you interested in exploring or investigating this semester? Use the method discussed in “Finding Research Gap” to identify at least two research gaps in the area or topic you selected.

I am interested in exploring the topic of information quality in upstream oil and gas operations, especially how poor data quality affects operational decisions, predictive modeling, and production performance. In upstream environments, data comes from heterogeneous subsurface, production, and operational systems, making it difficult to maintain trustworthiness across legacy and digital platforms.

Using the “Finding Research Gap” method, I identified the following research gaps:

- Gap 1: Few studies examine AI-ready data quality frameworks specifically designed for upstream operations, and limited work addresses how poor historical data propagates into biased or inaccurate models.
- Gap 2: There is still a need for integrated quality assessment methods that evaluate data trustworthiness across heterogeneous subsurface and production data sources.
- Gap 3 (most important): Research is limited on governance mechanisms that support sustained data quality in hybrid legacy-plus-digital operating environments.

These gaps suggest that more research is needed to understand how organizations can maintain reliable, trustworthy data in complex operational settings while also preparing those data assets for AI and advanced analytics.
