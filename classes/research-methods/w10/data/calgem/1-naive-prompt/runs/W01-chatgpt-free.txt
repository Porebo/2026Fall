2. Important technical deficiencies
There are several items I would prioritize.

A. Groundwater information

W01 identifies both the base of USDW and base of fresh water as N/A.

That is not adequate documentation for the two groundwater requirements in your specification. You need the applicable groundwater depths and TDS classifications, or an appropriately supported explanation of why the values are unavailable or inapplicable.

B. Cement behind casing

The diagram gives useful historical cement information:
- 9⅝-inch casing: 13–202 ft.
- 7-inch casing: 12–2,163 ft.
- Cement returns are reported at surface.
- Cement formulations, densities, additives and yields are described.
However, the diagram should explicitly identify the top and bottom of each cemented interval and how those depths were determined. The distinction between the theoretical cement placement interval and the actual verified cement top is important.

C. Directional well information

W01 identifies the well as directional.

Your specification requires a directional survey showing:
- Inclination measurements.
- Azimuth measurements.
- Bottomhole location.
- Surface location.
None of that survey information is provided in the drawing.

D. Measured depth versus true vertical depth

The diagram uses MD for its casing, perforation and formation depths, but does not provide corresponding TVD values.

This is a specific requirement under §1726.4.1(a)(2). A directional well needs both measurement references where applicable.

E. Wellhead equipment

Add the wellhead and valve assembly identification, including:
- Manufacturer/model.
- Pressure rating.
- Relevant valve assembly information.
F. Cement and abandonment information

The abandonment section is essentially blank:
- Abandonment tag depth: blank.
- Cement plug intervals: absent.
- Cement placement dates: absent.
- Plug verification methods: absent.
For an abandonment submission, this is particularly important.
3. The most important distinction: this is an abandonment submission
For a conventional oil and gas well, CalGEM's current operator guidance says the OG106 Notice of Intention must include an appropriate wellbore schematic.

CalGEM specifically identifies four things the schematic must show:
1. Casing intervals and sizes.
2. Perforation locations.
3. Cement plug depths inside casing.
4. Location of cement outside casing.

W01 already provides the first two.

The third is missing, and the fourth is only partially documented.

There is also a statutory requirement under Public Resources Code §3230 that the abandonment notice contain the total depth and complete casing record, including plugs.

5. One issue I want to bring to your attention
Your specification is based on §1726.4.1, which is specifically associated with underground gas storage projects.

It is not automatically the governing checklist for every conventional oil-well abandonment.

The broader checklist is useful, and I would retain it as an engineering quality-control standard. But I would distinguish:
- Abandonment submission requirements: OG106, applicable plugging requirements and the casing schematic.
- Underground gas storage casing diagram requirements: §1726.4.1, when applicable.
This distinction matters because we don't want to represent every item in the UGS regulation as a mandatory requirement for every ordinary producer abandonment.
