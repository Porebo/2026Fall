HW04 — Week 4 Assignment (Choosing a Research Design)

Evaluating Metrics (and Hypotheses) Assignment

Before you can collect and analyze data, you need to decide what to measure. Read the assigned study on COVID-19 vaccine acceptance among college students and explain how the authors defined and systematically collected their data.

Assigned article: Qiao et al. (2020), Vaccine acceptance among college students in South Carolina: Do information sources and trust in information make a difference? (medRxiv)

Assignment Instructions

Review the Assigned Study

Read the article carefully and pay attention to how the authors document their research study. Then answer the following questions:

  1. What questions did this research seek to answer in this study?
  2. Who were the participants in this study?
  3. What procedures did the researchers use to collect the data for their analysis?
  4. Describe the variables (attributes) that the researchers collected from their survey.
  5. Describe the different types of analysis that the researchers performed on the data collected from their survey.

Write up your responses and submit them to the Blackboard assignment folder when finished.
