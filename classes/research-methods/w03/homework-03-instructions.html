HW03 — Week 3 Assignment

Literature Review Assignment

Analyze the assigned 2024 systematic review, How Educators are Teaching AI in K-12 Education, and explain how the authors conducted and presented their literature review.

Blackboard assignment: Open Literature Review Assignment

Assignment Instructions

Review the Assigned Paper

Answer the following questions about the assigned systematic review:

  1. How many articles did the authors review for this paper?
  2. What corpus (collections, sources, or conference proceedings of papers) did they examine?
  3. What keywords did they use to find potential articles to include in their literature review?
  4. Describe how the authors organized and presented their findings from their literature review.
  5. What were the major findings and opportunities for further research that the authors discovered from their literature review?

Check the paper's references. Each listed reference should match a citation in the paper. Choose a major reference style and apply it consistently whenever you cite literature sources.

Submit your answers to the Blackboard assignment folder when finished.
