Assignment Instructions

Assess Your Analytical Skills

How are your analytical skills? The attached quiz from the BBC can help you assess how well you are able to interpret the results you obtain after submitting your data to your favorite statistical or visualization tool. Record your answers for each of the Skillswise questions and check your answers to see how you did. Correct responses are at the end of the quiz, but try not to peek until you are done.

You do not need to report your scores for the various levels. Write a reflection on the exercise that answers these questions:

  1. How comfortable do you feel with your present level of analytical, statistical, and visualization skills? What do you feel are your strongest areas? What areas would you like to improve?
  2. For the areas you would like to improve, what actions do you plan to take over the next 12 months to strengthen your analytical and technical skills? You do not need to rely on coursework alone. Online resources such as Coursera, edX, Udacity, DataCamp, Pluralsight, and YouTube can help you achieve your learning goals.

Write up your reflection on this exercise and submit it to the assignment folder.
