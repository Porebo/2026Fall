Study Design Assignment

Plan a research design for the topic you intend to use in your research proposal. Explain how the study will answer your research question with valid, appropriate methods.

Assignment Instructions

Describe Your Study Design

Write responses to each of the following questions about your proposed research:

  1. What problem, issue, challenge, or opportunity will your research examine?
  2. What research objectives, questions, or aims will address the problem?
  3. What evidence or data will you need to answer those objectives or questions?
  4. What methods will you use to collect the data?
  5. What is your tentative plan for analyzing the data?

Write up your responses and submit them to the Blackboard assignment folder.
