HW05 — Week 5 Assignment

Study Design Assignment

The research (study) design is a strategy for answering your research question. A good research design helps ensure that your conclusions are valid and align with your research objectives, that your data is high quality and reproducible, and that you are using appropriate analytical tools and techniques for answering your questions.

Reference: Scribbr — Research Design

Assignment Instructions

Answer Questions About Your Research Area

For this assignment, you will be answering the following questions about the research area that you plan to use for your research proposal:

  1. Describe the problem (i.e., issue, challenge, opportunity) that you intend to examine for your research proposal.
  2. What are the research objectives/questions/aims you plan to examine to help address the problem you identified?
  3. What types of evidence (data) do you need to collect in order to answer your research objectives/questions/aims?
  4. What methods do you plan to use to collect this data?
  5. What is your tentative analysis plan?

Write up your responses to these questions and submit them to the assignment folder.
