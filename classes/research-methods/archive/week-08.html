Learning Module

Week 8 Writing Your Research Proposal

Research requires good writing skills. This week students will learn about what needs to go into a research proposal, a research report, along with formatting guidelines for UALR Thesis/Dissertations. We will also talk about other types of research writing and tips for how students can use the web to answer questions about what is expected of the final product (e.g., formatting).
