Course Books

E-Books Folder

This folder contains the reference books that we will use this semester.

  • Ranjit_Kumar_Research_Methodology_A_Step.pdf (PDF)
    This is a free 3rd edition of Ranjit Kumar's book on Research Methodology. This will be the main textbook that we will be using for this course.
  • Engineering Research Methodology: A Computer Science and Engineering and Information and Communication Technologies Perspective
    Another good resource on research methods.
  • Art_of_Discovery_Oliver2.pdf (PDF)
    Ever wonder what distinguishes a great researcher? This classic text presents a treasure trove of insights that can be applied to any discipline.
  • Answering Questions with Data: Introductory Statistics for Psychology Students
    This e-book from the Open Textbook Library is an excellent reference for reviewing and understanding the statistical fundamentals that you will need for conducting research.
  • Student-Guide-to-AI-final-081224.pdf (PDF)
    Some guidance on the appropriate use of artificial intelligence in your courses at UALR.
