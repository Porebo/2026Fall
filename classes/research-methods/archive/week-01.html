Learning Module

Week 1 Overview and Introduction

Introduction to Research Methods and the Information Quality Field. The objectives for this week include (1) Introductions and Welcome to Class; (2) Tour of the Course Shell and Syllabus; (3) Overview of the Information Quality Discipline: Past, Present, and Future; (4) Discussion of What is Research/What are the different research types?; and (5) How does Research get done (i.e., what are the Typical Steps of the Research Process) - See the Ranjit Kumar book for one example.

Instructions

Welcome to INFQ 72203 Information Quality Theory for Fall 2025. For this first assignment, answer these five questions and submit your responses to the assignment folder when done. You are welcome to use Internet resources to help craft your answers.


  1. There are many definitions for research. In a sentence or two, how would you explain to someone what is the meaning and purpose of research? Note: If you got your response from the Internet, that is fine. Just include the URL to document your source.
  2. Just like there are many different answers to what is research, there are also many ways to characterize the different steps in the research process. Use the Internet (ex. you can google the phrase "research process steps" and check out the images) to find a research process diagram that you like and that helps you to understand the steps in conducting research. Copy the image and remember to include the URL to document your source.
  3. What is your desired career goal?
  4. How will research help you to achieve that career goal?
  5. What area/topic/issue/problem/challenge(s) are you interested in exploring/investigating this semester? Use the method discussed in "Finding Research Gap" to identify at least two research gaps of the area/topic that you are interested in.
