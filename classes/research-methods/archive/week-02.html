Learning Module

Week 2 Formulating a Research Problem

This week we will look at the challenge of coming up with a good problem to investigate. The objectives for this week include (1) Learning how to develop a good problem statement and (2) Learning how to use the literature review to refine, validate and support the problem statement.

Instructions

Students must choose the focus of their research proposal as early as possible.  For this assignment, you need to write up a one-page thought plan that includes the following sections.


  • Introduce your topic.  For example, start with a compelling statistic, a provocative question, or a striking fact related to your topic. Then, provide the broader context of your research field, gradually narrowing the focus to your specific subject.
  • Give as much background and context as you can at this point, such as who could have an interest in the topic (e.g., scientists, policymakers), how much is already known about the topic, what is missing from this current knowledge, what new insights your research will contribute, and why you believe this research is worth doing
  • Outline your problem statement and research questions.  Hint: Check out Writing Strong Research Questions from https://www.scribbr.com/research-process/research-questions/

Submit your write-up to the assignment folder when done.


Need help finding a topic? Remember to review the materials from Week 1 and  Week 2 to help you generate possible ideas.
