    This website has a lot of nice templates that researchers can use to get ideas on how to ask a variety of questions related to business interactions. Check out https://www.questionpro.com/survey-templates/business-to-business-demographics/ for some examples of business demographic questions.
  • Google Forms for Survey Tool
    Web surveys are very common and it is important to have a good web survey tool. There are vendor tools like SurveyMonkey, JotForm, TypeForm, SurveyPlanet, Qualtrics, Zoho Survey, QuestionPro and many more. Many of these companies have a free tier that is very limited. More extensive survey features/functions require a paid subscription. At UALR, students and faculty have access to Google Forms which is a good starting point for designing your survey.

Instructions

The research (study) design is a strategy for answering your research question. A good research design helps to ensure that your conclusions are valid and align with your research objectives, that your data is high quality and reproducible, and that you are using appropriate analytical tools and techniques for answering your questions. There are many designs out there to support the many different types of research situations. You can see some examples of study designs at https://www.scribbr.com/methodology/research-design/.


For this assignment, you will be answering the following questions about the research area that you plan to use for your research proposal.


  1. Describe the problem (i.e., issue, challenge, opportunity) that you intend to examine for your research proposal.
  2. What are the research objectives/questions/aims you plan to examine to help address the problem you identified?
  3. What types of evidence (data) do you need to collect in order to answer your research objectives/questions/aims?
  4. What methods do you plan to use to collect this data?
  5. What is your tentative analysis plan?

Write up your responses to these questions and submit them to the assignment folder.
