Instructions

Before you can collect your data and analyze it, you need to figure out what you are going to measure. Check out this article on vaccine acceptance among college students - https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2020.12.02.20242982v1.full-text


Read it carefully and pay attention to how the authors document their research study. In order to prove or disprove their hypotheses, the authors needed to define and systematically collect their data. Based on this article, consider the following questions.


  1. What questions did this research seek to answer in this study?
  2. Who were the participants in this study?
  3. What procedures did the researchers use to collect the data for their analysis?
  4. Describe the variables (attributes) that the researchers collected from their survey?.
  5. Describe the different types of analysis that the researchers performed on the data collected from their survey.

Write up your responses and submit them to the assignment folder when done.
