Learning Module

Week 6 Institutional Research Board

This week we will be looking at the Federal Regulations governing Human Subjects Research. In addition, students will learn about research ethics.

Class Materials

Week 6 Class Materials

Here you will find any handouts and presentation materials for this week's unit.

  • Ethical Guidelines and the Institutional Review Board – An Introduction
  • UALR IRB
    We will take a tour of the UALR IRB, procedures, dates, forms, and templates that students should be familiar with.
  • Survey Ethics
    This article is a nice preview of our upcoming discussion on the IRB and Human Subjects Research.
  • Example IRB Review Request.pdf (PDF)
  • Guidelines for Adult Consent Form for Surveys (PDF)
  • Example IRB Response Letter (PDF)
  • CITI Training Website
    All CIS PhD students need to complete their ethical requirement. Completing the required training modules from CITI will enable students to have the documentation that they need to get this requirement satisfied on their DegreeWorks Profile.
  • Responsible Conduct of Researchers
    For today's class, we are going to be looking at the Responsible Conduct of Researchers case studies to discuss some of the tricky situations that can occur when conducting research.

Instructions

Anytime you do research on the UALR campus, you need to go through the UALR IRB to make sure that your research complies with all federal regulations. For this assignment, you will be completing:


  • the steps listed at https://ualr.edu/irb/home/required-training/ to earn your certificate in Human Research Training (Course Learner Group: Group 1. Social and Behavioral Research Investigators and Key Personnel.
  • the steps listed at https://ualr.edu/irb/home/required-training/ to earn your certificate in Responsible Conduct of Research (You can pick the version of RCR Training that is most applicable to your research interests).

Once you have completed the certificates, submit your two course completion reports listing the expiration date and the completed modules to the assignment folder.
