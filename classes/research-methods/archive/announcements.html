Project Presentations


Hello all,


As described on our blackboard portal, you are expected to prepare a research proposal as a course assignment by the end of the semester. As part of this, we ask that every student prepare a brief presentation, and optionally present it in class to receive comments and feedback. Since some of you are attending this class asynchronously, attending a live session and presenting your idea will be optional. Whether you are able to present your proposal or not, you should  submit the presentation slides by Nov. 9th.


Please review the task descriptions on our Blackboard page and let us know if you would like to attend one of the presentation sessions to be held on Nov. 9th, 16th or 30th. Based on your responses, we will schedule the sessions and assign each student to one of these dates. The deadline to let us know is this Friday,  October 30th. You may e-mail sxdagtas@ualr.edu or nxwu@ualr.edu about this.


Please let us know if you have any questions/concerns. Thank you!


Serhan Dagtas
